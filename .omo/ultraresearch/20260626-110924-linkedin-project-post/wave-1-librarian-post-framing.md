## Key Findings

- The best post frame is first-person, concrete, and evidence-led.
- Avoid broad AI hype, "10x" language, or "fully autonomous" claims.
- Use a structure: context, method, verification, evidence, learning.
- For Korean/English professional audiences, calm and direct wording is safer than promotional copy.
