# Wave 1 Digest: LinkedIn Official Profile Mechanics

Worker: Librarian the 10th  
Access date: 2026-06-26

## Key Findings

- Headline and the profile introduction card are edited from the intro section; headline is the top-of-profile search-visible positioning field.
- About is a distinct long-form profile section, not the headline. LinkedIn guidance says it should express mission, motivation, and skills, ideally in one or two paragraphs.
- Featured can show work samples, authored posts, articles, external links, images, documents, presentations, and videos. It is strong for evidence but obeys profile visibility and is not itself search-discoverable.
- Skills can be added and reordered; LinkedIn supports up to 100 skills.
- Open to Work supports visibility choices: all LinkedIn members, recruiters only, or only you. Recruiter-only visibility is not guaranteed to be hidden from every current-company recruiter, but it is the privacy-oriented option.
- Public profile visibility is separately configurable from signed-in LinkedIn member visibility.
