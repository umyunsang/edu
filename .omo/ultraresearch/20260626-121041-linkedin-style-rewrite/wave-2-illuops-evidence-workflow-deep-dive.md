# Wave 2: IlluOps Evidence Workflow Deep Dive

IlluOps should be described as a planning/evidence/reference surface, not a shipped runnable application.

## Safe Claims

- Evidence-first workflow.
- Public/private proof boundary.
- Claim-support mapping.
- Planning/reference repository.
- Local verification is separate from public proof.

## Avoid

- "Released"
- "Production-ready"
- "Fully verified"
- "Publicly validated"
- "Runnable app"
- Treating `.omo` logs as public social proof.

## Hook

> AI work is only worth posting when the claim and the proof link are close enough for someone else to inspect.
