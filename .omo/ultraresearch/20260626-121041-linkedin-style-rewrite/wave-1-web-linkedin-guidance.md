# Wave 1 Web: LinkedIn Platform Guidance

## Key Findings

- LinkedIn official help describes posts as feed updates with a 3,000-character ceiling and link previews when a URL is pasted.
- LinkedIn official Page guidance recommends 3-5 relevant hashtags for Page posts, while current practitioner/vendor guidance tends to favor 1-3 for personal posts.
- Official and industry guidance converge on substance: post text should provide value even if it links elsewhere.
- Native documents/carousels and multi-image posts are repeatedly recommended by industry analyses for performance, but this task is text-only unless images/slides are later requested.
- Link placement is disputed. The safest user-facing rewrite is self-contained text with one direct artifact link, plus "더 자세한 링크는 댓글에" variants if reach optimization is more important than immediate proof.
- Engagement prompts should be genuine questions, not bait.

## Sources

- LinkedIn Help: sharing links and posts supports preview generation and allows up to 3,000 characters.
- LinkedIn Business Pages best practices: use 3-5 relevant hashtags when relevant.
- LinkedIn Page posting best practices one-pager: clear, actionable, not overly promotional content performs best.
- Buffer LinkedIn algorithm guide: link posts should still deliver value in the body; hooks matter; replies to comments matter.
- Hootsuite LinkedIn algorithm guide: native content is favored; relevance can keep older posts circulating.
