## Executive Summary

The best assignment topic is "음식물쓰레기가 환경에 미치는 영향" because it allows concrete current status, causes, everyday examples, and solutions in simple language. UNEP reports that 1.05 billion tonnes of food waste were generated in 2022 at retail, food service, and household levels, and households accounted for 60 percent. UNFCCC/IPCC-aligned sources state that food loss and waste account for about 8 to 10 percent of annual global greenhouse gas emissions. EPA explains that prevention is the best pathway and that landfilled wasted food creates methane.

For a memorized Korean answer, use only one headline number and avoid technical phrasing. The safest simple argument is: food waste wastes not only food, but also the water, land, labor, and energy used to make it; when it rots, it can produce gases that worsen climate change; therefore the solution starts with buying, cooking, and serving only what we need.

## Findings By Theme

- Current situation/problem: 2022 global food waste was 1.05 billion tonnes at retail, food service, and household levels. Households were 60 percent. Source: UNEP 2024.
- Cause: overbuying, poor planning, date-label confusion, large portions, buffet/restaurant leftovers, and strict appearance standards.
- Example: home delivery leftovers, school meal leftovers, and vegetables thrown away because of appearance are easy examples.
- Solution: plan meals, buy only needed amounts, serve small portions first, reuse leftovers, donate edible surplus, and use pay-by-weight/RFID systems where available.
